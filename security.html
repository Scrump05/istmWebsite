Article Section: Famous Cybersecurity Incident

The first Cyber Security incident in history occured on November 2, 1988. It was called the Morris Worm and was caused by a graduate student named Robert Tappan Morris from MIT. The worm created by Morris affected 6000 out of 60,000 computers and many prestigious universities, military and research facilities which casued estimated damages of over $100,000 worth of damages which sooner went into the millions. This case made Congress pass the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act of 1986.

Types of Malware

  1. Virus: Software written with malicious intent to cause annoyance or damage.
  2. Trojan Horse: Harmful code disguised as legitimate software that makes users want to install it.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial Gain: Uses malware to access data from the recipients system to gain access to their finacial data or to demand funds from them.
  2. Hacktivism: Hacks that are motivated by political or social causes to attack systems and networks.

Prevention Strategies

  • Knowledge of Phishing Emails: Not clicking on suspicious links or downloading attachments from unknown sources.
  • Unique Passwords: an 8-12 character password with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
